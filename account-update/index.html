What should you have noticed?

Unexpected account change

Confirm whether you requested the update. An unsolicited change notice needs independent verification.

Sender identity

Check the actual sender and verify the notice with your support team through a known channel.

Destination URL

Open the corporate identity portal from a trusted bookmark rather than an unexpected message.

Credential request

Do not provide credentials or personal information in response to an unverified account-update request.
